UX process

Five steps I follow on every app and website I design.

  1. 01

    Understand

    I talk to users and study the problem before I draw anything.

    InterviewsSurveysCompetitor review
  2. 02

    Define

    I turn what I learned into personas, user journeys and one clear problem to solve.

    PersonasJourney mapsInformation architecture
  3. 03

    Design

    Wireframes first, then high-fidelity screens in Figma built on a design system.

    WireframesUI designDesign system
  4. 04

    Test

    I put prototypes in front of real users and fix whatever confuses them.

    PrototypesUsability testsIteration
  5. 05

    Deliver

    I hand off clear specs so developers build exactly what was designed.

    SpecsDeveloper handoffDesign QA
