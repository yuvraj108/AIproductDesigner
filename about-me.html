About me

Hi, I’m Yuvraj — a product designer who starts with people.

I’m a Product Designer based in Bangalore. I’m endlessly curious about people: I listen closely, find the real problem behind what they say, and design simple products that solve it.

Over the past year I’ve worked as a Product Designer (Trainee) at Bridge UX Design Studios and a UI Designer at Studio Sensorium — running research, building wireframes and Figma prototypes, testing with real users and handing off clean specs. I enjoy healthcare most, like redesigning PharmEasy for senior citizens, where good design means people feel safe.

Outside work, I travel solo, ride bikes, play football, hunt for unique places and edit videos. They all teach me the same thing: notice the small details that make an experience feel right.
